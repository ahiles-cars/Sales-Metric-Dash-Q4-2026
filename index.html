Method. Coverage = open October website opportunities ÷ (VP regional target − websites sold); a region at or above target reads "At/over goal". Websites are opportunities with Product Offering = Website, counted once per opportunity (not per quote line). Sold = unique New Product website opportunities closed in October per the Actuals tab. Open = every other stage. Expected (weighted) coverage discounts open opportunities by stage — Closing 80%, Presentation 40%, Follow-Up 25%, Discovery 10% — shown in the hover detail. Regions roll up per the Hierarchy tab by Client Partner; only individuals listed there are counted, with three attribution rules: (1) Andrew Pelc (Northwest manager) as Client Partner counts under Northwest; (2) John David Scozzaro (Texas manager) as Client Partner counts under Texas; (3) opportunities owned by an Inside rep count under Inside even when the Client Partner field shows another name (Rob Karamanlian on opportunities owned by Mike Egan or Mohammad Alam). Sold counts New Product website opportunities only (Existing Product amendments and upgrades are excluded) and uses the credited Seat on the Actuals tab; open opportunities are not filtered because the pipeline pulls carry no New/Existing flag. Targets come from the Target tab (October Website Target by Region). Source: CPPipelineTemplate10162026.xlsx — Pipeline10162026 tab (all October close dates), with the Pipeline1052026, 1072026 and 10122026 tabs as earlier pulls for the movement notes. Movement notes compare each region with the first pull of the month (Oct 5) on the same rules. Sold in each snapshot = New Product website closes on the Actuals tab dated on or before the snapshot date; earlier snapshots are restated with the current Actuals tab, so they can differ from previously published figures.
